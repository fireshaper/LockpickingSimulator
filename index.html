      Welcome to the Lockpicking Simulator

      Practise picking pin tumbler, dimple and disc detainer locks, with a cutaway view that shows what each pin or disc is doing as you work.

      This is only a simulator. It models how locks behave, but it is not a real lock: the feel, tolerances and forces are approximations, and skill here will not transfer exactly to the real thing.

      Build a lock

      A pin tumbler lock. Choose how many pin stacks it has, then choose the driver for each stack, click a key pin to make it serrated, and pick a length for each key pin: 1 is the shortest, 9 the longest. A longer key pin means a deeper cut in the key, and less lift to set.
